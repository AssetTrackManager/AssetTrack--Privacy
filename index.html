1. Introduction

AssetTrack is an asset management application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when you use our application.

2. Data Controller

AssetTrack is operated by Simion Cristian Ionuț, based in Romania.

Contact: SimionCristianIonut1994@gmail.com

3. Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address and password.
  • Asset information: asset names, categories and photographs uploaded by users.
  • User-entered information: descriptions, expenses, repairs, investments and other information entered by users.
  • Subscription information necessary to manage payments and subscriptions.

AssetTrack does not request the physical address or geographical location of your assets.

4. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage user accounts.
  • To provide asset management features.
  • To store and display assets, photographs and financial records.
  • To manage subscriptions and payments.
  • To provide customer support and maintain service security.

5. Third-Party Services

Bubble: provides application infrastructure, hosting and database services.

Stripe: processes subscription payments and related payment information.

Payment card details are handled by Stripe and are not intentionally collected directly by AssetTrack.

6. Data Storage and Security

Information is stored using infrastructure provided by Bubble. Reasonable measures are taken to protect personal information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Account Deletion and Data Retention

Users can delete their accounts directly through the AssetTrack application.

When an account is deleted, associated assets, photographs, expenses, investments and other user-created records are deleted. The associated Stripe subscription is also cancelled.

Some information may be retained where required by applicable law.
